# Compensation Bands 2026
Band L5 (Senior Engineer) salary range is 42 to 58 lakh INR. Band L6 (Principal) range is 60 to 85 lakh INR.

## Annual review
Merit increases are capped at 12 percent and are approved by the compensation committee in April.
